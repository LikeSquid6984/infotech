In 1947 India could not build a satellite, a fighter jet or a chip. Today it lands on the Moon's south pole, exports defence equipment to more than eighty countries, and runs the world's busiest payments network. This is that story — and the twenty-one years left to finish it.

01 · The Journey

One century, drawn as a wheel

The Ashoka Chakra has twenty-four spokes. Here it becomes a clock: the hundred years from 1947 to 2047 wrapped once around the circle, every milestone pinned where it truly falls. Watch what happens after 2008 — the markers crowd together, because that is when India's pace changed.

1947 Independence

Achieved Still ahead

  1. 1947

    Independence

    The clock starts on 15 August. Every target that follows is measured against 2047, the hundredth year.

  2. 1969

    ISRO is founded

    Set up on 15 August 1969 — the same date, twenty-two years on. India had no rocket of its own at the time.

  3. 1975

    Aryabhata, the first satellite

    Built in Bengaluru, launched on a Soviet rocket. The spacecraft was Indian; the ride was not. Closing that gap took another fifteen years.

  4. 1998

    Pokhran-II

    Five tests in May 1998. Sanctions followed — and with them a long push to build at home what could no longer be bought abroad.

  5. 2008

    Chandrayaan-1 finds water

    India's first lunar mission detected water molecules on the Moon — a result that redirected lunar science worldwide.

  6. 2014

    Mars, on the first attempt

    The Mars Orbiter Mission entered orbit on 24 September 2014. No other country had reached Mars on its first try.

  7. 2016

    UPI launched

    A public payments rail, free to use. Ten years on it carries about 81% of India's digital payments.6

  8. 2022

    INS Vikrant commissioned

    The first aircraft carrier designed and built in India. Roughly 76% of it was sourced domestically.

  9. 2023

    Chandrayaan-3 lands at the lunar south pole

    23 August 2023 — the first landing anywhere near it. The ChaSTE probe drilled ten centimetres down and found sub-zero soil below eight centimetres, in two distinct layers.7

  10. 2024

    Aditya-L1 reaches the Sun's L1 point, SpaDeX docks

    India's first solar observatory settled into orbit in January. By December, India became the fourth country able to dock two spacecraft — the technique a space station depends on.7

  11. 2025

    Operation Sindoor

    7 May 2025, a tri-service response to the Pahalgam attack — and the first real test of India's networked air defence. See how it worked →

  12. 2026

    The first commercial Indian chips ship

    Kaynes Semicon's Sanand plant reached commercial production in March 2026 — fourteen months after breaking ground.5

  13. 2047

    Viksit Bharat

    Not reached, and not guaranteed. Everything above was built. Everything after is still a plan. What the plan says →

03 · Defence

Operation Sindoor, and the systems behind it

On 22 April 2025, terrorists killed twenty-five Indians and one Nepali citizen at Pahalgam. What followed was a response — and, for the first time, a full test of technology India had spent a decade building.

  1. 22 April 2025

    Terrorists attacked civilians at Pahalgam. Twenty-five Indians and one Nepali citizen were killed.1

  2. 7 May 2025 · 01:05 hrs

    Operation Sindoor began. Nine sites of terrorist infrastructure in Pakistan and Pakistan-occupied Jammu and Kashmir were struck. The Ministry of Defence stated that no Pakistani military facilities were targeted.1

  3. The days after

    Drones and loitering munitions came the other way. They were stopped less by guns than by software — two Indian systems that turn scattered radars into a single shared picture.8

How a networked air defence actually works

Tap a stage. Akashteer does this for the Army, IACCS for the Air Force.

Detect. Radars, sensors and observation posts each catch part of what is coming. On its own, every one of them holds an incomplete — and slightly different — story.

Fuse. Akashteer and IACCS merge those feeds into one live picture, so a single drone reported by four radars is understood as one drone, not four.

Decide. Every track is classified and handed to whichever weapon is best placed to take it. Automating this is what shrinks the response from minutes to seconds.

Engage. The assigned system fires. Through Operation Sindoor this chain intercepted drones, missiles, micro-UAVs and loitering munitions.8

04 · Vision 2047

What the last twenty-one years are for

Viksit Bharat means a developed India by 2047, the hundredth year of independence. NITI Aayog names four groups it has to work for — and an economy of about $30 trillion to pay for it.9

युवा

Yuva · The young

Education and real work for the largest working-age population on earth.

गरीब

Garib · The poor

Extreme poverty ended, not merely reduced.

अन्नदाता

Annadata · Farmers

Higher yields and incomes that hold up against a changing climate.

नारी

Nari · Women

Full participation in the economy, not partial.

Seventy-nine down. Twenty-one to go.

Those seventy-nine years produced a lunar landing, a home-built carrier, a payments system the world now copies, and the first Indian chips. The last stretch of the wheel is still drawn as a dashed line — because nobody has walked it yet.
